deepfilternet3.onnx - DeepFilterNet 3 noise suppression model.

Model and weights: DeepFilterNet by Hendrik Schroeter et al. (https://github.com/Rikorose/DeepFilterNet),
dual-licensed MIT OR Apache-2.0. Streaming ONNX export: torchDF (https://github.com/grazder/DeepFilterNet).
Source file: huggingface.co/kimtos-labs/denoiser-dfn3 (denoiser_model.onnx,
sha256 fe5eb64fa2e4154c83f8e4935e82871c850c154387ee892e0ab65fe179e7d8c9).

StreamLite changes (numerically identical output, verified to -107 dB): ONNX Runtime CPU-only fused operators
(FusedConv, FusedMatMul) were expanded back to standard ONNX Conv/activation/MatMul, and the attenuation-limit
If node was replaced by an equivalent Where, so the model also runs on graphics cards through DirectML.

deepfilternet3_low.onnx (Low VRAM) is made from deepfilternet3.onnx by tools/make_dfn_lean.py: the state vector's
SplitToSequence/SequenceAt and the NonZero-based "first frame" check are rewritten as standard Split and
ReduceSum/Equal (identical output), and the three GRU layers are stored and run in 16-bit floats (output within
50 dB of the full model).
